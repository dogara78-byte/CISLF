    <h3>Troop 303</h3>
    <p>Troop 303 is open to boys ages 11 to 17.</p>
    <p>Meeting time is 6:30&ndash;8:00 p.m. almost every Tuesday at the American Legion Post 331. An active unit, we have weekend outings 11&ndash;12 times yearly in addition to a weeklong summer camp.</p>

    <h3>Troop 331</h3>
    <p>Troop 331 is open to girls ages 11 to 17.</p>
    <p>Meeting time is 6:30&ndash;8:00 p.m. almost every Tuesday at the American Legion Post 331. An active unit, we have weekend outings 11&ndash;12 times yearly in addition to a weeklong summer camp.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="unit-block">

    <h3>Crew 303</h3>
    <p>Crew 303 is open to both boys and girls ages 14 to 20.</p>
    <p>Meets twice monthly at American Legion Post 331. Our goal is to provide two or more 7+ day adventures per year in addition to weekend outings or day trips. We attend high adventure bases in addition to Create-Your-Own-Adventure type experiences.</p>
